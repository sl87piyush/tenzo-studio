# 30 / 60 / 90 day SEO implementation plan

Priority weighs business impact, effort, confidence, and dependencies. Numeric growth targets wait until Search Console and enquiry baselines are recorded. “#1” is an aspiration, not a forecast.

## Months 4–6

- Build a small library of approved case studies and useful buyer resources from real project questions. Link each resource to the relevant service page. Keep a manageable publishing cadence rather than publishing for word count.
- Seek legitimate local partnerships, referrals, association profiles, and relevant media stories. Record the relationship and resulting links/mentions. No purchased links, review gating, or bulk directory work.
- Refine web vs Maps tracking. Review whether Jodhpur services need stronger local proof and whether any page is competing with another for the same query cluster.

## Months 7–12

- Reassess Jaipur and Udaipur first, then Delhi and Mumbai. Expand only if there is real delivery capacity, distinct buyer demand, and unique local value; otherwise strengthen general service pages and case studies.
- Consider separate automation, landing-page, or paid-media pages only when GSC queries, qualified leads, service scope, and proof show a distinct buyer job.
- Revisit information architecture, mobile field data, structured data, and content freshness quarterly. Retire or consolidate weak pages rather than multiplying near duplicates.
